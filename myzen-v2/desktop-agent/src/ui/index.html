What is recorded

  • Punch in/out, breaks and time you log.
  • While punched in and not on break: the name of the app in front, the site host if available, and seconds since your last mouse/keyboard input.

What is never recorded

  • What you type, window titles, page contents, clipboard.
  • Anything while you are punched out or on a break.
  • Screenshots, unless your company has switched them on for your team (currently: off).
